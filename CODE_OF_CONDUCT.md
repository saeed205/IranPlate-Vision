# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Being respectful of differing opinions and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users
- Being patient with newcomers, and with non-native speakers of English or Persian

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Sharing real RTSP credentials, plate data, or footage of identifiable people
  in issues or pull requests

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and
when someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer via the contact on
[@saeed205](https://github.com/saeed205). Reports are handled privately.
Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and may temporarily or permanently ban
anyone for behaviour they judge inappropriate.
